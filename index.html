The Problem

A Two-Fold Problem

Sports analytics are not designed for consumers — and returns for gambling are bad.

-5%
Average ROI for sportsbook sports betting

-8%
Average ROI for prediction markets sports betting

Markets thrive on low-information gambling. The house has a massive analytical advantage over consumers.

Not Aimed at Consumers

Current analytics target leagues, teams, and gambling companies — not individual bettors. B2B focus with enterprise-grade pricing and complexity.


Aimed at High-Skilled Users

Existing B2C tools require Python/R expertise, statistical knowledge, and DIY model building. Academic exercises, not actionable insights.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are your models? ▾

Our AI/ML models are continuously trained on historical data, team metrics, and situational factors. We publish regular performance analyses so users can evaluate accuracy transparently.

Is sports betting legal in my state? ▾

Sports betting legality varies by state. Better Bettor provides analytics and predictions — we are not a sportsbook. We recommend checking your local regulations before placing any bets.

What sports do you cover? ▾

We're launching with college basketball (NCAA) and expanding to NBA and NFL. The All-Access Pass includes all current and future sports as they become available.

What's the difference between the free and premium models? ▾

The free Elo model provides transparent, easy-to-understand predictions. The premium AI/ML model uses advanced machine learning for higher accuracy, including spread and total points predictions.

Can I cancel anytime? ▾

Yes. Simple monthly subscription with no contracts, no hidden fees. Cancel anytime from your account settings.
